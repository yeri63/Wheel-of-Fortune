<p>Play the TV game Wheel of Fortune on a Cisco IP Phone. All the puzzles are song lyrics from popular films or artists my daughters enjoy. To help come up with the phrase and what letters to guess, a hint with the film or artist is shown when the game begins.</p>
<p>Here's a few of the puzzles hidden within the game...</p>

How to Play:

1. Note the Hint to give you an idea of the song lyric hidden on the board. This will get easier as more letters are revealed during game play.
2. The player must spin the wheel to start the game.
3. If the wheel lands on a dollar amount, the player can pick a consonant letter which could be in the puzzle. If that letter is indeed in the puzzle, that dollar amount is awarded for every match found.
4. if the player balance is at least $250, the player can pick a vowel. If the vowel appears more than once, the player only pays $250.
5. The player can continue to pick vowels until all of the vowels have been discovered, or  funds drop below $250. At that point the wheel must be spun again before proceeding.
6. When there are only constants remaining, the wheel must be spun before guessing each letter.
<p>The object of the game is to guess all letters without going Bankrupt (which could occur on any spin), and earn the most by the end of the game. Try to beat your last score or that of friends or family members playing this same game.</p>
